WHY THERE IS NO FOURTH CASE
  Under the contemporaneous service rule tau_t = rbar*b_t + g_t the
  primary balance is zero at every date, so
      B_t = (1 + i^ss) B_{t-1}   =>   detrended B is CONSTANT.
  Contemporaneous taxation therefore PINS terminal detrended debt at its
  initial value. "Contemporaneous tax + permanently higher terminal debt"
  is not a hard cell to compute; it is an empty one. A fully independent
  factorial interaction is NOT identified here and is not reported.
