===== D. STRUCTURAL VERDICTS =====

V1  IS THE SUPERSTAR STATE IDENTIFIED BY ITS SINGLE MOMENT?
    wealth_concentration_fit searches (mult, p_in) over a 3 x 3 grid
    with p_out held fixed at 0.0600 and selects the cell whose top-1 pct
    share is closest to a SINGLE target (0.3300). Two free parameters
    against one moment is an a-priori count, and section B counts it
    that way. Whether the direction is actually FLAT is a different
    question, and it is measurable: beta is recalibrated to the debt
    target inside every config, which constrains the pair, so the
    counting argument alone does not settle it.
    MEASURED by that driver:
      9 configs solved; 3 tie on the top-1 pct target within 0.0200.
      among the ties, the top-10 pct share spans 0.0137 and the
      gini spans 0.0065.
      => NOT the binding problem. The tied configs agree on
         the untargeted moments to within the tie band, so on
         this grid the single moment is locally sufficient and
         the a-priori count OVERSTATES the slack here.
         Two limits, both real: a 3 x 3 grid is coarse enough
         that ties are scarce, and local sufficiency at one
         point is not global identification. It does mean the
         slack in section B sits elsewhere -- see V6 and V2.
      selection rule in force: single-moment (top-1% only) -- TOP10_TARGET not transcribed

V2  THE WEALTHY-HAND-TO-MOUTH SHARE IS A STRUCTURAL ZERO.
    Model value: 0.0000.
    This does not need a data slot in order to fail. With phi = 1 the
    whole dividend d*k arrives as LIQUID income, so a household rich in
    the tree never runs its liquid buffer down, at any beta, lambda or
    bbar. The share is zero BY CONSTRUCTION rather than by calibration,
    and any positive external value therefore rejects it. The two
    parameters governing this margin -- phi and bbar_liq -- are exactly
    the two carrying the DECL class in the two-asset block. That is not
    a coincidence: the margin the model cannot reproduce is governed by
    the parameters nothing external pins down.

V3  ZETA IS DISCIPLINED AT THE EDGE OF ITS RANGE, NOT AT ITS CENTRE.
    The KVJ mapping dln(spr)/dln(b) ~ -zeta puts the point estimate
    near zeta = 1 and the range near [0.55, 2.05]. The benchmark uses
    zeta = 2.0000. What matters is not zeta itself but the elasticity it
    implies AT the calibrated liquid position, which is damped by the
    Stone-Geary shift: -zeta*b/(b+bbar) = -1.8174.
    KVJ counterpart, as transcribed in calibrate_convenience_kvj: -1.0274.
    Model minus KVJ point estimate: -0.7900.
    KVJ RANGE, from the same transcription: [-2.0548, -0.5479].
    The model elasticity is INSIDE that range, 16% of the way
    up from the elastic end. So the benchmark is not rejected by
    the KVJ evidence; it is disciplined at the ELASTIC edge of it,
    which is a claim about where in the range the paper sits and
    not a claim that the paper is outside it. Note that the point
    estimate alone, graded against half the range WIDTH, would
    have called this a failure -- the range is asymmetric and the
    symmetric approximation manufactures the rejection.
    Either way the paper should report the ZETA = 1.0 rerun alongside
    the benchmark, not instead of it, and say which it prefers and why:
    the sensitivity is what a reader needs, and it is cheap.

V4  THE CLIMATE BLOCK IS ENTIRELY DECLARED.
    Only D0 is tied to an external source, and then only as a
    three-point sweep across damage literatures; theta_g, delta_g,
    phi_D, psi_inc and scale_floor have no external target at all.
    Every abatement-effectiveness statement is conditional on theta_g,
    which is why the damage-dividend term should be read as an
    elasticity with respect to a declared parameter rather than as a
    measured magnitude.

V5  IDENTIFICATION AND DISCRETIZATION ARE SEPARATE FAILURES.
    Section B measures the first; Gate 11 measures the second. Passing
    Gate 11 would not add a single external target, and a parameter
    with no external target has no external target at any resolution.
    Neither result can be used to excuse the other.

V6  CHI IS CALIBRATED IN A DIFFERENT ECONOMY FROM THE ONE IT IS USED IN.
    chi_b is fitted in main_twoasset_ownership -- the FRICTIONLESS
    companion, lambda = 1 -- to S_b = 0.30, and then transplanted into
    the infrequent-adjustment economy, where beta is refitted to the
    same moment. Two instruments therefore chase ONE target across two
    economies. The transplant is defensible -- the driver states the
    reason, that the friction roughly doubles precautionary wealth and
    puts the level out of chi's reach -- but it is a modelling choice,
    not an identification, and section B counts the moment once.
    The WTARGET option adds total wealth as a second moment and makes
    the pair (beta, chi) genuinely two-instrument; it is not what the
    shipped benchmark runs, and the paper should say which it reports.

V7  THE CONVENIENCE YIELD AND THE EQUITY PREMIUM ARE ONE NUMBER.
    The liquid FOC in this economy is
        chi_b v'(b') = u'(c) [1 - (1+r_b) q/(q+d)],
    so the tree's excess return over bonds IS the convenience yield:
    the tree is the only alternative asset, and nothing else can absorb
    a return gap. The manuscript says as much in the two-asset section.
    Model value: 5.9472 pp -- the same number under both names. The two
    ways of writing it agree to the digit, which is the point:
      100*[(q+d)/q - (1+r_b)] = 5.9472      (called a convenience yield)
      100*[ d/q  -  r_b     ] = 5.9472      (called an equity premium)
    The consequence is a restriction, not a calibration choice. The
    Treasury-premium literature and the equity-premium literature
    measure objects that differ by roughly an order of magnitude, and
    this model has ONE number to offer both. It cannot sit in both
    ranges, so a reader is entitled to know which one the paper is
    claiming to match and to see the other named as a cost.
    Neither data slot is transcribed yet, so the SIZE of the tension
    is not measured here. The identity above holds regardless, and
    it is the identity -- not the gap -- that the paper must own.
    Transcribing DATA.convenience_pp and DATA.equity_premium turns
    this verdict into a number; it will not turn it into a pass.
