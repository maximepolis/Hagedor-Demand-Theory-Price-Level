READING.  The sign mapped here is the sign of the LUMP-SUM
semi-elasticity in the CERTIFIED ONE-ASSET economy.  It is not a
statement about the two-asset economy, and the two axes the referee
named that live only there (lambda_adj, iota_H) are declared unpopulated
above rather than approximated.  Within each node the split shows where
the response comes from: where sh_dst is near one, the paper's MPC and
constrained-share discipline is speaking to a small part of the object,
and cov_tlt is the part it speaks to.  A positive fraction close to one
is evidence the sign is not a knife-edge in THESE dimensions; it is not
evidence about the dimensions that were not swept.
